Module 2

Your First Heart. Create a Single Complete Heart Motif.

This is where it clicks. You'll take everything from Module 1 and use it to create one complete heart from start to finish. Every curve, every point, every line of texture — built knot by knot in a logical sequence you can follow without second-guessing. You'll end this module holding something that looks like jewelry.

  • How to map the heart shape before you start so you always know where you're going
  • The exact cord length formula so you never run short mid-motif
  • A simplified technique for creating symmetry by feel — no measuring, no fussing
  • You'll complete one full heart motif, ready to build on in Module 3
Module 3

Building the Collar. Connect Multiple Hearts Into a Necklace Shape.

One heart is a motif. Seven hearts connected with intention is a necklace. This module teaches you how to join your pieces so the collar curves naturally at the neck, stays flat when worn, and looks deliberate and professional. You'll see the full shape of your necklace emerge for the first time.

  • The joining technique that keeps each heart angled correctly as the collar curves
  • How to plan your layout before you start connecting so nothing needs to be undone
  • Learn how cord direction and tension between motifs affect the drape of the finished piece
  • How to adjust the necklace length to fit any neckline or wearing preference
  • You'll finish with a complete collar of seven connected hearts, minus the centerpiece
Module 4

The Centerpiece. Craft the Statement Pendant Heart.

The centerpiece pendant is the soul of this necklace. It's larger, more intricate, and features the open teardrop design with a single pearl drop that makes the whole piece look like fine jewelry. This module breaks it down into a clear sequence so it never feels overwhelming.

  • How to scale up a heart motif without losing the proportions that make it look right
  • The technique for creating the open teardrop frame at the base of the pendant
  • How to attach the pearl drop so it hangs cleanly and doesn't shift when worn
  • Discover how the centerpiece's weight and structure anchor the entire necklace at the front
  • You'll complete the full statement pendant, ready to attach to your collar
Module 5

Finishing Touches. Complete, Clasp, and Wear Your Necklace.

The final step. You'll attach the centerpiece to the collar, add the clasp and finishing knots, and trim everything clean. By the end of this lesson, your necklace is done. Put it on.

  • How to attach the centerpiece pendant to the collar so it sits centered and secure
  • The finishing knot sequence that locks everything in place without adding visible bulk
  • How to attach a lobster clasp or adjustable sliding closure for a clean, professional finish
  • A simple technique for sealing cord ends so they stay tidy wash after wash
  • You'll end this module wearing your completed Heart Necklace — finished, beautiful, yours
