Transferring Your Butterfly

How to recreate the butterfly using a simple grid, even if you've never drawn before.

Module 3

The Peel and Reveal Technique

How to apply drawing gum along the mosaic lines, the simple method behind the clean mosaic effect.

  • How to trace the mosaic joints with confidence, even if your lines aren't perfectly straight
  • Exactly how long to let the drawing gum dry so every joint stays protected before you start painting
  • The quick visual test that tells you the drawing gum is fully dry so you don't start too early and ruin your lines
Module 4

Painting by Color Zone

How to paint freely by color, creating smooth transitions, vibrant gradients, and that signature rainbow mosaic look.

  • The color zone method that lets you paint freely. No careful edging, no second-guessing every stroke
  • The simple blending trick that creates smooth rainbow transitions without muddy colors
  • The one simple step to complete before moving to the next color zone so your colors stay clean and don't bleed into each other
Module 5

The Big Reveal

Peel off the drawing gum, reveal the crisp mosaic lines, and get your artwork ready to display.

  • The exact moment to start peeling and why rushing it can undo everything
  • How to clean up the mosaic lines so they look crisp, white, and perfectly defined
  • How to finish and present your painting so it's something you'll actually want to hang on your wall
