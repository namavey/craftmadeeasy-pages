Now the real magic begins. You’ll learn exactly how to apply drawing gum along the lines that will eventually become the crisp white separators in your painting. This is the step that makes the final reveal so jaw-dropping.

Free Painting. Filling Each Zone Without Fear Of Going Outside The Lines.

Here’s where everything clicks. Because of the drawing gum, you can now paint freely, loosely, even messily… and your lines will still come out perfect. It feels almost too easy. This module teaches you how to paint zone by zone with total freedom.

  • Why painting “too carefully” ruins your piece (and what to do instead)
  • The blending trick that makes each color zone look soft and professional
  • How to load your brush just right so the paint flows without streaks
  • Discover the right order to paint your wings so nothing smudges
  • You’ll finish this module with all your zones painted, ready for the big reveal

The Big Reveal. Peeling Off The Gum And Unveiling Your Finished Butterfly.

This is the moment you’ll never forget. Once everything is dry, you’ll slowly peel the dried gum off the canvas… and watch every single white line appear at once. It looks like magic. It feels like magic. And you made it happen.

  • You’ll finish this module with a completed, frame-ready rainbow butterfly painting
