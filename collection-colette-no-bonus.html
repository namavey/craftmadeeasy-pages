Module 3 — The Statement Brooch

Breaking free from straight lines into flowing, organic designs

Here's where your collection begins to develop its own personality. You'll move beyond simple symmetry and learn to create graceful, flowing shapes that naturally draw the eye. It may look impressive, but by now you'll have all the skills you need to create it with confidence.

  • How to "draw" with thread: building graceful curves freehand, with total confidence
  • The secret to organic composition so your piece flows naturally instead of looking stiff
  • Working on a brooch base (a brand-new little skill that opens up a whole world of pieces)
  • How to balance a design that isn't symmetrical and still looks intentional and elegant

Module 4 — The Coordinated Earrings

The little details that turn pieces into a collection

A real collection is coordinated. Now you'll create earrings that echo your bracelets and bring the whole set together. Small pieces, big payoff… and a beautiful exercise in precision.

  • The mirroring technique that makes both earrings come out perfectly matched (no more "close enough")
  • How to work small and neat, so tiny pieces still look crisp and refined
  • Attaching ear hooks cleanly, so your earrings look effortless, not fiddly
  • The repetition trick that builds real precision into your hands... almost without trying

Slip these on, glance in the mirror, and watch your handmade collection suddenly feel complete.
