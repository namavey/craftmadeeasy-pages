Cookie Policy — Craft Made Easy

Last Updated: March 12, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Craft Made Easy uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website to enhance user experience, analyze performance, and support our marketing efforts. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect and use personal information.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, unless you disable them through your browser or our cookie consent tool.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember user preferences, analyze behavior, and tailor advertising. Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies (deleted when you close your browser)
  • Persistent cookies (stored for a defined period)
  • First-party cookies (set by our website)
  • Third-party cookies (set by external services we use)

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable features such as secure checkout, user authentication, access to your student dashboard, and essential navigation. These cookies cannot be disabled, as the website cannot operate without them.

2.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies

  • Google Analytics — Tracks traffic, page views, time on site, and navigation patterns.
  • Microsoft Clarity — Provides heatmaps, session recordings, scroll tracking, and interaction analysis. Data collected is pseudonymized and used to improve both our website and Microsoft's services.

Analytics cookies are loaded only after you give consent via our cookie banner.

2.3 Advertising & Marketing Cookies

Meta Pixel (Facebook Ads) — Used to track conversions, measure the effectiveness of ads, build audiences based on website behavior, and optimize ad delivery. Advertising cookies are not loaded without your explicit consent.

2.4 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language settings, saved progress, and login preferences. They enhance usability but are not strictly necessary.

3. Cookie Consent Management (CookieYes)

We use CookieYes as our cookie consent management platform to comply with GDPR, ePrivacy, and advertising platform requirements. This tool allows us to display a cookie banner on your first visit, collect and store your consent choices, block non-essential cookies until consent is given, categorize cookies, and allow you to modify or withdraw your consent at any time.

You can revisit your cookie preferences at any moment using the "Cookie Settings" link available in the footer or banner.

4. Third-Party Cookies We Use

  • Meta (Facebook)
  • Google Analytics
  • Microsoft Clarity
  • Systeme.io (for course access and checkout)
  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)

We do not control their cookies and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

  • Through our cookie banner: You can accept, refuse, or customize cookie categories.
  • Through your browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Instructions for popular browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) are available in their help sections.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and user experience of our website.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or updates to our Services. New versions will be posted on this page with the updated date.
